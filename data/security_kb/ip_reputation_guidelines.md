# IP Reputation Assessment Guidelines

## Scoring interpretation

- 0-34: Low risk (monitor)
- 35-74: Medium risk (add containment controls)
- 75-100: High risk (immediate containment and escalation)

## Analyst actions

- Cross-check IP with external reputation providers.
- Correlate with internal firewall and authentication logs.
- If high reputation score aligns with brute-force indicators, prioritize host isolation.
